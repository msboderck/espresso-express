How the lane works

Three stops. The whole point is that you never turn off the car.

Stop 1 · Order

Order ahead or at the board

Tap your usual in the app before you leave the house, or tell us at the speaker. Regulars get recognized by plate.

Stop 2 · Pull

Ground and pulled while you roll

Your shot starts the moment the order lands. By the time you reach the window, it's in the cup, not in a queue.

Stop 3 · Window

Grab, tap, go

Card, phone, or app credit. We hand you the cup lid-down so the foam survives the on-ramp.
